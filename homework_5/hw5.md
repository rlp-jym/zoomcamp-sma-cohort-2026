# Market and scope

Philippines (PSE), high volatility names (ATR threshold), hold for weeks (trailing stop, no targets).

# Investment thesis

PSE is long only, so the expectation is for price to appreciate within the given period. This bias is enforced by the lack of options locally. Staying in cash is a genuine and preferred position during cyclical downturns.

# Potential edge

Our local market is still inefficient but at the expense of liquidity. No competition with HFT and algorithms. 

# Data and sources

The exchange itself, which regularly publishes EOD quotes. Sufficient for swing trading on higher timeframes. Indicators: technical and news; foreign funds analysis (liquidity is 50% foreign); local macros are delayed and not practical. 

# Research approach

PSE is a stock picker’s market. Signal and rules are technical-based to protect from fundamental bias. Model: not covered but I am looking into the practical application of Bayes theorem. I am not a fan of regression when it comes to stock selection. Material disclosures have the potential to transform behavior, making past data obsolete.

# Validation and risks

Simple benchmark is holding index trackers like PSE:FMETF or AMEX:EPHE. I prefer fixed income interest rate as alternative, the difference expressed as a multiple. To make it worth the risk, I want 5x the net return, regardless of market performance. PSEi is -2% CAGR or -20% for the last decade. At net of 6% p.a., that would be a minimum of 30% portfolio gain end of year after fees and taxes.
